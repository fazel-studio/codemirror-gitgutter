@fazelstudio/codemirror-gitgutter

Git-blame-style diff gutter + VSCode-like peek view, driven purely by a baseline string.

Click a colored marker in the gutter to open the peek view. Use Alt-F3 / Shift-Alt-F3 to jump between changes and Ctrl-Shift-Backspace (Cmd on macOS) to revert the change under the cursor. The Stage button logs a fake staging event below.
